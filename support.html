Getting started

  1. Download Guard Lock for Mac, open the zip, drag Guard Lock into your Applications folder and open it.
  2. Install the free Guard Lock app on your iPhone.
  3. On the Mac, click Pair iPhone App…. On the iPhone, scan the code it shows.
  4. Click Send Test Alert on the Mac. Your iPhone should show it within seconds.

Common questions

Alerts don't arrive. Check that both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive on, that notifications are allowed for Guard Lock on the iPhone (Settings › Notifications › Guard Lock), and that the Mac shows "Alerts are on" in its window.

No photo with wrong-password alerts. Turn on Photo on wrong password in the Mac window and allow camera access when macOS asks.

Lock Mac is slow or doesn't work. Allow notifications for Guard Lock on the Mac (System Settings › Notifications › Guard Lock). This lets it get the command straight away.

Moving to a new Mac. In the old Mac's Guard Lock window, open License and click Deactivate License…, then enter the same key on the new Mac.

Lost your license key. It's in the receipt email from Lemon Squeezy. You can also find it at app.lemonsqueezy.com/my-orders.
