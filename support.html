Setting up

  1. Download Guard Lock for Mac (free), unzip it, drag Guard Lock into Applications and open it.
  2. Get the Guard Lock iPhone app from the App Store.
  3. On the Mac, click Pair iPhone App…, then scan the code it shows with the iPhone.
  4. Click Send Test Alert on the Mac. Your iPhone should show it within a few seconds.

When something's off

No alerts are coming through.

Check three things: both devices are on the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on, notifications are allowed for Guard Lock on the iPhone (Settings › Notifications › Guard Lock), and the Guard Lock window on the Mac says "On, watching for unlocks".

Wrong-password alerts come without a photo.

Switch on Photo on wrong password in the Guard Lock window on the Mac, then allow camera access when macOS asks.

Lock Mac is slow, or does nothing.

Allow notifications for Guard Lock on the Mac as well (System Settings › Notifications › Guard Lock). That's the path the lock command takes to arrive straight away.
